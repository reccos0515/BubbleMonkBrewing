Finally using a bunch of wild picked oranges! I zested them and peeled them so that I wouldn't have the pith in the brew, supposedly that's where most of the pectin and bitter flavor is.

## Ingredients
 - 3 lbs. raw honey
 - ?? lbs. Wild Oranges, zested and peeled
 - 2 grams K1-V1116 yeast
 - 3 tbsp black tea
 - 1/2 tsp pectic enzyme
 - 1/2 tsp Fermaid-O Nutrient
 - 1 gal Filtered tap water

## Recipe
 1. Sanitize equipment using Star-San
 4. Simmer Cherries at ~160 degrees farenheight for 20 min (removes any bacteria just in case)
 6. Add to fermenting vessle.
 7. Add honey/water and stir
 8. Allow to cool to 65 deg F
 9. Add Fermaid-O to the fermenter, use the Tosna calculator for further nutrient additions.
 10. Pitch yeast according to the package with the addition of Go-Ferm and add to the fermenter
 11. Top with water until there's a reasonable amount of room left for the fermentation (take initial gravity reading)
 12. Seal using a sanitizer filled airlock and set in a cool dark place
 13. After fermentation Stabilize, Backsweeten the mead according the the process in <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/about">Resources</a>
